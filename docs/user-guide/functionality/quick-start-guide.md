# Quick Start Guide

Welcome to [LandExplorer](https://landexplorer.coop/). We’ve put together a simple user guide to help you get started. It walks through the basics of [LandExplorer](https://landexplorer.coop/), so you can quickly find your way around. Once you've familiarised yourself with this video why not have a look at the below:

* [Land Ownership](left-toolbar/data-layers/#land-ownership) — discover who owns land and explore ownership layers
* [Data Layers](left-toolbar/data-layers/) — overlay OS maps, ﬂood zones, agricultural land classiﬁcations, SSSIs, and social housing data
* [Drawing Tools](left-toolbar/drawing-tools.md) — measure areas and add annotations to the map
* [Creating a Map](creating-a-map.md) — save your findings in their own map
